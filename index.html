Problem:

Water flows through a cylindrical pipe of internal diameter 2 cm, into a cylindrical tank of base radius 40 cm, at 0.4 m/s.

Determine the rise in water level in the tank in 30 minutes.

Solution:

Pipe Radius (r): 1 cm = 0.01 m
Flow Rate (v): 0.4 m/s
Tank Radius (R): 40 cm = 0.4 m
Time (t): 30 min = 1800 s

Volume In = π * r² * v * t
Volume In = π * (0.01)² * 0.4 * 1800
Volume In = 0.072π m³

Rise in Level (h) = Volume / (π * R²)
h = 0.072π / (π * 0.4²)
h = 0.072 / 0.16
h = 0.45 m = 45 cm
